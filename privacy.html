Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

"Plain Science" is a personal automation tool operated by the owner of the Plain Science YouTube channel. It is used only by that owner to upload the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

Data the application accesses

With the owner's consent, the application uses Google OAuth to access the owner's own YouTube account through these scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload - to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions and schedule.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly - to check the status of the channel's own videos.

How data is used and stored

The application uses this access only to upload and check the channel owner's own videos. The OAuth credentials are stored privately on the owner's own computer and in the owner's private automation environment. We do not collect, sell, share or disclose Google user data to any third party, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google API Services

The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Plain Science YouTube channel.
